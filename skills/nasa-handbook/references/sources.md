# Sources

What each source contributed. Open the AOH scans when you want to see a real
page; they settle most "would NASA do this?" questions.

- **Apollo Operations Handbook, Block II Spacecraft, Vol. 1 (SM2A-03-BLOCK II-(1), 1969)**,
  page structure, running head, numbered paragraphs, change bars, thumb tabs,
  figure codes, the Mission / Basic Date / Change Date / Page footer, block and
  schematic diagram style.
  Sequential systems: https://www.ibiblio.org/apollo/ApolloProjectOnline/Documents/SMA2A-03-BLOCK%20II%20Volume%201%2019691015/aoh-v1-2-09-seqsys.pdf
  Docking (line art with callouts): https://www.ibiblio.org/apollo/ApolloProjectOnline/Documents/SMA2A-03-BLOCK%20II%20Volume%201%2019691015/aoh-v1-2-13-dock.pdf
  Controls and displays: https://www.ibiblio.org/apollo/ApolloProjectOnline/Documents/SMA2A-03-BLOCK%20II%20Volume%201%2019691015/aoh-v1-3-cont+disp.pdf
- **NASA Graphics Standards Manual, NHB 1430.2 (Danne & Blackburn, 1975/1976)**,
  Helvetica as the post-Apollo house face, grids, outline boxes around technical
  diagrams, one- and two-colour printing limits.
  https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2015/01/nasa_graphics_manual_nhb_1430-2_jan_1976.pdf
- **collectSPACE, "Font style used on Apollo cuff checklists"**: Letter
  Gothic in caps for Apollo and early Shuttle Flight Data File material.
  https://www.collectspace.com/ubb/Forum14/HTML/001393.html
- **GSFC Engineering Drawing Standards Manual (X-673-64-1F)** and **KSC-GP-435**:
  title blocks, revision blocks, drawing titles as "NOUN, MODIFIER — TYPE".
  https://s3vi.ndc.nasa.gov/ssri-kb/static/resources/NASA%20GSFC-X-673-64-1F.pdf
  https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20205010487/downloads/KSC-GP-435_Vol1_RevH-Final-Signed.pdf
- **Graphic Design Stack Exchange, 1960s NASA technical diagram fonts**: Futura
  for titles, Letter Gothic and Leroy lettering for diagram text.
  https://graphicdesign.stackexchange.com/questions/24978/1960s-technical-charts-sans-serif-all-caps-font
- **Fonts**: Routed Gothic, traced from a Leroy lettering template (SIL OFL 1.1),
  https://webonastick.com/fonts/routed-gothic/ ; Letter Gothic Code, a modified
  URW Letter Gothic (Aladdin Free Public License: free to use and share, not to
  sell), https://github.com/jamesxmcintosh/LetterGothicCode . Licences are in
  `assets/fonts/`.
