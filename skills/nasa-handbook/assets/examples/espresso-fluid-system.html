PRESSURIZED EXTRACTION UNIT
2.12.1

INTRODUCTION.

The pressurized extraction unit (PEU) prepares espresso in the surface habitat galley. At the 56.5 kPa (8.2 psia) cabin pressure water boils at 84 °C (183 °F), below the 93 °C (199 °F) brew temperature, so the PEU brews at sea-level pressure in a sealed circuit and releases the extract to the cabin only after cooling it (figure 2.12-1).

2.12.2

FUNCTIONAL DESCRIPTION.

With cb PEU HTR closed, the thermoblock heats to 93 °C and the HTR RDY talkback goes gray. GROUP LOCK clamps the portafilter to the group head. BREW starts pump P1, which draws potable water through extract cooler HX1 and drives it through the thermoblock and the puck at 10.0 bar absolute. The extract collects in the sealed sump, gives up its heat to the incoming water in HX1 and leaves through BPV-2 (figure 2.12-2).

2.12.2.1

Back-Pressure Hold.

Valve BPV-2 holds the sump at 101 kPa (14.7 psia) and opens only while extract temperature T2 is at least 4 °C below the cabin boiling point: T2 ≤ Tsat(56.5 kPa) − 4 °C = 80 °C (176 °F).
